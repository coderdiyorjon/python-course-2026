# Learning Resources — Day 02

## Digital logic, voltage levels and binary states

- [MIT OpenCourseWare — Using Voltages Digitally](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-004-computation-structures-spring-2017/pages/c2/c2s1/) — kuchlanishning raqamli mantiqda qanday talqin qilinishi, bitta threshold yetarli bo'lmasligi va ishonchli raqamli abstraksiya haqida.
- [All About Circuits — Logic Signal Voltage Levels](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/digital/chpt-3/logic-signal-voltage-levels/) — low/high darajalari, threshold va noise margin haqida tushuntirish.
- [MIT — Computing Hardware](https://fab.cba.mit.edu/classes/865.24/topics/computing/computing/hardware.html) — tranzistorning kalit sifatidagi roli va raqamli sxemalarning fizik asosi.

## SRAM and DRAM

- [Computer History Museum — Semiconductor Memory](https://www.computerhistory.org/revolution/memory-storage/8/311) — SRAM, DRAM, tranzistorli latch, kondensator va refresh o'rtasidagi farqlar.
- [Computer History Museum — The Silicon Engine Glossary](https://www.computerhistory.org/siliconengine/glossary/) — DRAM tuzilishi va refresh atamalari.

## Number systems and conversions

- [MIT OpenCourseWare — Number Representation and Hexadecimal](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-004-computation-structures-spring-2017/pages/c1/c1s1/) — binary va hexadecimal orasida to'rt bitli guruhlash nega ishlashini tushuntiradi.
- [SATHEE — Encoding Schemes and Number System](https://sathee.iitk.ac.in/ncert-books/class-11/computer-science/chapter-02-encoding-schemes-and-number-system/) — binary, octal va hexadecimal konvertatsiya misollari.
- [NTU — A Tutorial on Data Representation](https://www3.ntu.edu.sg/home/ehchua/programming/java/datarepresentation.html) — pozitsion formulalar va asoslar orasida konvertatsiya qilish usullari.

## Floating-point numbers

- [Python Documentation — Floating-Point Arithmetic: Issues and Limitations](https://docs.python.org/3/tutorial/floatingpoint.html) — `0.1` nima sababdan binary floating-point formatida aniq saqlanmasligi, `0.1 + 0.2` va sonlarni taqqoslash haqida.
- [IEEE 754-2019 — Standard for Floating-Point Arithmetic](https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/754/6210/) — floating-point formatlari va arifmetika uchun standart tavsifi.

## O'qituvchi uchun eslatma

Materialni tushuntirishda fizik signalni mantiqiy qiymat bilan tenglashtirmang: `0` va `1` — kodlashdagi qiymatlar; ularni ifodalovchi fizik holat qurilma va sxema turiga bog'liq. SRAM/DRAM taqqoslashida bu yerda berilgan xususiyatlar umumiy arxitektura tavsifi ekanini unutmang. Floating-point xatti-harakatini ko'rsatganda Python'ning rasmiy hujjatlariga tayaning.
